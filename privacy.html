Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

KeiBot Studio Pribadi ("the App") is a private personal tool operated by its owner to manage the owner's own YouTube channels via the YouTube API Services and Google APIs.

Data accessed

The App accesses only the Google account data authorized by the owner, such as YouTube channel information, video upload and management data, and related metadata, solely to render, upload, schedule and manage the owner's videos.

Use of data

Data is used only for the owner's channel operations. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train models.

Storage and retention

Access tokens are stored on the owner's private server and are used only to operate the App. The owner may revoke access at any time via their Google Account security settings, after which the App can no longer access the data.

Contact

Questions: contact the app owner via the user support email shown on the Google consent screen.
